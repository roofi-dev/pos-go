## Verify the built result

- **Contrast:** body and placeholder text at least 4.5:1; large text at least 3:1.
- **Depth:** shadows have a real offset and soft blur; avoid decorative zero-offset halos.
- **Spacing:** keep related controls close and separate distinct groups generously. Maintain more space above headings than below.
- **Type:** clear body/display scale and weight steps; prevent overflow and test real copy at each breakpoint.
- **Motion:** use purposeful, coherent motion from an already-visible default; respect reduced-motion preferences.
- **States:** cover hover, focus, disabled, loading, error, empty, and success states, plus real content and working controls.
- **Browser surfaces:** theme selection, caret, scrollbar, focus ring, and data numerals to fit the interface.
- **Coverage:** all design brief requirements should be present and discoverable quickly.

## Avoid design shortcuts

- Do not use generic card grids as the default page structure or add decorative section numbers and eyebrow labels without a content reason.
- Avoid gradient text, decorative glass/blur, hard offset shadows outside a deliberately brutalist design, and repeated rounded boxes with no hierarchy.
- Do not use a system display face as the unique brand voice; select an intentional, available font and test its loading/fallback behavior.
- Do not use emoji or Unicode glyphs as a substitute for a coherent icon system, or as final product photography.
- Do not imitate food photography with geometric masks, gradients, or hand-drawn SVGs. Use real imagery or omit the image.
- Derive surfaces and palettes from the actual use environment, not category stereotypes.
- Keep claims and configuration grounded in supplied product facts.

This floor checks mechanics; the project's `DESIGN.md` remains the visual authority.
